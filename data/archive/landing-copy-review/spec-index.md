## Overview

The landing page is the single conversion surface for Spec Doc. Every distribution channel — Reddit posts, Twitter threads, Product Hunt, direct links — funnels through this page. A weak headline or unclear CTA burns traffic that took real effort to generate. This capability treats the landing page as a conversion machine and audits every layer: copy strength, trust signals, technical correctness (email form endpoint, OG tags), and performance (mobile layout, load speed).

Spec Doc has no users yet, so traditional social proof ("10,000 teams trust us") is off the table. Instead, the approach leans on builder credibility signals — concrete output metrics like "18 epics shipped in 42 hours" — that demonstrate the product works by showing what it produced. This is more honest and more compelling to the early-adopter audience who can smell fake testimonials.

The outcome is a landing page where every element has a job, every job is measurable, and nothing is left to "looks fine to me" judgment. Copy changes are specific and testable. Technical fixes are verifiable. The page either converts or it doesn't, and after this capability ships, the bottleneck moves upstream to traffic quality — not page quality.
