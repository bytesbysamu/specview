## Overview

AI tool directories are passive distribution channels — submit once, get discovered indefinitely. Unlike Reddit posts or Twitter threads that decay within hours, directory listings compound over time as users search for tools by category. For a solo founder with no marketing team, directories are the highest-leverage distribution activity: low effort per listing, permanent presence, and SEO backlinks that improve organic search ranking.

This capability covers drafting and submitting Humaniz.me to 10 targeted directories: Product Hunt (prep only — launch is a separate event), There's An AI For That, Futurepedia, AI Tool Guru, TopAI.tools, Toolify.ai, AItoolslist, SaaS AI Tools, Uneeq AI, and Creatie.ai. Each submission requires a tailored description, consistent positioning, correct categorization, and a logo/screenshot asset package.

The strategic question this capability resolves is positioning: Humaniz.me should be listed as an "AI text humanizer" or "AI text rewriter," not "voice-to-text rewriter" (which misrepresents the product). The directories that matter most are the ones that drive actual TestFlight downloads and paying conversions, not just backlink juice. This capability includes a lightweight tracking mechanism to measure which directories send real traffic so future products can skip the dead ones.
