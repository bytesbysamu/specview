# 🎯 Epic: Specview SaaS Go-to-Market

## Business Value

Specview is a working AI spec generator with a live landing page, $29/mo Pro tier, and a self-referential origin story ("0 human code lines, 36 projects generated by the tool itself") that is genuinely interesting to the audience that pays for developer tools. The product exists. What is missing is the path from "stranger lands on specview.io" to "stranger pays $29." Closing that path is what this epic delivers.

The market is solo developers, indie hackers, and small teams who already pay for AI tooling and start more projects than they finish. They live on Hacker News, r/SideProject, and dev Twitter — channels reachable by a single well-timed Show HN post rather than a sales motion. A confident launch into that audience is the cheapest, highest-yield distribution event available to a zero-audience founder, and the self-referential angle is the kind of hook those readers reshare.

The revenue thesis is narrow but testable: if 50+ signups produce at least one $29 conversion in the first month, the pricing model is viable and the funnel can be optimized against real data. Without launching, every retention and pricing question stays a guess. This epic exists to replace those guesses with signal.

## Scope

### What This Epic Covers
- **Stripe live + usage limits enforced** — `@check_usage_limit` deployed, real Checkout URL, paid conversions possible
- **Phase 4 reliability gate** — timeouts, error envelopes, polling retries verified before any public traffic
- **Activation analytics** — landing → signup → first-spec funnel instrumented end-to-end
- **Email capture on landing page** — recover bounced traffic from launch-day spikes
- **Show HN launch** — single primary distribution event, self-referential framing, live demo path verified
- **Secondary amplification** — r/SideProject crosspost and Twitter/X relay content prepped to ride HN traction

### What This Epic Does NOT Cover
- ❌ **No-auth playground** — Product change, not GTM; revisit only if free→paid stalls post-launch
- ❌ **$99/mo team tier** — No validated demand; revisit after a team-context inbound
- ❌ **$9 one-time 10-pack** — Pricing experiment; revisit only if free hit-rate is high but subscription conversion is low
- ❌ **Twitter/X as primary channel** — No existing audience; secondary amplification only
- ❌ **Dev.to / Hashnode SEO post** — High ROI but slow; out of launch-week scope
- ❌ **Product Hunt launch** — Gated on post-launch onboarding polish
- ❌ **Retention model** — Unresolved by design; needs live data first

## Success Criteria

- ✅ Live Stripe Checkout completes a real $29 payment end-to-end
- ✅ `@check_usage_limit` blocks the 4th spec for a free-tier user with a clear upgrade prompt
- ✅ Spec generation under concurrent load (10+ simultaneous requests) returns either success or a structured error envelope — no stuck spinners
- ✅ Funnel dashboard shows: landing visits, signups, first-spec-generated, free-limit-hit, Pro-conversion
- ✅ Landing page captures email from non-converting visitors
- ✅ Show HN post is live with a verified demo path and a defensible framing of the "0 human code lines" claim
- ✅ 50+ real signups within the first week of the Show HN post
- ✅ At least 1 paid conversion within the first 30 days
