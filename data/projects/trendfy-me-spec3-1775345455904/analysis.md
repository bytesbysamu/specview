## The Core Problem

Fashion trend discovery is broken. Users who want to stay current with clothing trends face a fragmented landscape: scattered across Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, fashion blogs, and runway coverage. By the time a trend filters through these channels to mainstream awareness, early adopters have already moved on. The information asymmetry between fashion-forward individuals and average consumers creates a perpetual game of catch-up.

The deeper issue is translation. Even when users identify a trend (say, "quiet luxury" or "coastal grandmother"), converting that abstract concept into actionable wardrobe decisions requires expertise most people lack. What specific pieces define the trend? What's worth investing in versus what's fleeting? How does a trend adapt to different body types, climates, and budgets?

Consider: It's like having a weather forecast that says "precipitation likely" without telling you whether to grab an umbrella or a snow shovel.

---

## Symptoms

Users experience:

- Spending hours scrolling social media trying to identify what's "in"
- Buying trend pieces that feel dated within weeks
- Confusion about which trends suit their personal style
- Analysis paralysis when shopping due to trend overload
- FOMO-driven purchases that never get worn
- Difficulty distinguishing lasting trends from micro-trends
- No clear signal on trend timing (emerging vs. peaking vs. declining)
- Inconsistent outfit cohesion when mixing trend pieces with existing wardrobe
