## Overview

The current LinkedIn profile positions Sam as a Java/Angular developer — an identity that's 2+ years out of date. Every profile visit from a potential user, collaborator, or investor sees a signal that doesn't match reality. The rewrite repositions the profile around what's actually happening: an AI builder who built a methodology (Spec Doc) that turns brain dumps into shipped products, with 18 epics generated and executed in 42 hours as proof.

This is a 15-minute execution task with outsized leverage. LinkedIn is the professional credibility layer — it's where people land after seeing a post, a product, or a mention. The headline and about section are the two highest-traffic elements on any profile. Getting them right means every future profile visit converts at a higher rate, whether the goal is users for Humaniz.me, credibility for the methodology, or inbound interest.

The key decision is positioning purity: full indie builder (no employer mention) vs. hybrid (day job provides context). The analysis below resolves this based on audience and conversion goals.
