## The wedge — Claude, not GPT

AI detectors (GPTZero, Turnitin, Originality.ai) were trained on GPT-3.5/4 token patterns. Claude produces different perplexity/burstiness signatures and gets detected at materially lower rates. This is our defensible angle: "the humanizer that uses Claude's natural patterns." Not "bypass detection" — "natural writing protection."

Even after humanization, detectors still catch 61% of fully AI text. The cat-and-mouse is real but Claude-native is a genuine edge right now.
