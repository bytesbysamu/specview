# 🔍 ex-girlfriend — Analysis

## The Problem
Considering restarting a 4-year relationship with ex-girlfriend that ended ~5 months ago (December 2025). Decision point is next Wednesday. Two in-person meetings have happened since the breakup, most recent last Sunday.

## Hard Constraints
- Decision deadline: next Wednesday (2026-05-13) — also the 4-year anniversary of original relationship start
- Relationship history: 4 years together, broken up December 2025 (~5 months apart)
- Only 2 post-breakup meetings to draw signal from; latest was last Sunday

## Open Questions
- Why did the relationship end in December? — (a) resolvable conflict, (b) incompatibility, (c) external circumstance. Without this, "is it a good idea" is unanswerable.
- What changed in the 5-month break? — (a) one person grew, (b) both did, (c) nothing changed and loneliness is driving this.
- What did last Sunday's meeting actually signal? — (a) mutual desire to restart, (b) one-sided interest, (c) ambiguous / unspoken.
- Is the Wednesday date symbolic (anniversary) or a real conversation deadline? — symbolic pressure can force a premature yes/no.
- Has the original breakup reason been addressed, or is restart assuming it will resolve itself?

## Dependencies & Sequencing
- Breakup-cause clarity blocks restart-viability assessment
- Honest read of her intent (not yours projected onto her) blocks the Wednesday conversation
- Wednesday conversation blocks any "restart" — restart is not a unilateral decision

## Explicitly Out of Scope
- Picking a future relationship structure (move in, marriage, etc.) — too far ahead; trigger to re-scope: only after 1–2 months of confirmed restart
- Reliving / relitigating old fights in this analysis — not a planning input; trigger: handle in the actual conversation, not the spec
- Anniversary symbolism as a decision driver — date coincidence is not signal; trigger: if Wednesday is chosen *only* because of May 13, defer the decision
