## Overview

Cold DM outreach is the highest-signal user acquisition channel available at pre-traction scale. One person who tries humaniz.me because you DMed them is worth more than 100 directory impressions — they have the exact pain the product solves, they self-selected by tweeting about it, and their feedback is grounded in a real workflow.

This capability defines the complete outreach system: how to find the right people on Twitter/X (search queries, keyword patterns, recency filters), what to say (three message variants under 280 characters each, helpful tone, no sales pitch), how to track results (who was contacted, who replied, who installed), and how to measure whether the channel works (response rate, install rate, retention of DM-sourced users vs. organic).

The target is 50 DMs over 2 days. At a conservative 10% response rate, that yields 5 real testers — people who arrive with the exact problem humaniz.me solves and can give feedback that shapes the product. If the channel converts, it becomes a repeatable weekly ritual. If it doesn't, the failure data tells you whether the problem is targeting (wrong people), messaging (wrong pitch), or product (right people, tried it, didn't stick).
