# Swiss Finance B2B Cold Outreach Campaign

i work in finance in zurich. i know the industry, i know the jargon, i know the pain points. i want to build a small SaaS product targeting small swiss financial firms — external asset managers (EAMs), family offices, independent financial advisors. these are firms with 2-30 people. there are roughly 2000 of them registered with FINMA under the FINIA license.

my problem isn't building the product. i can code anything. my problem is distribution. how do i actually get in front of these people and get them to pay CHF 300-900/month for something?

what i know:
- FINMA register is public and downloadable, gives me every licensed firm in switzerland
- these firms are swimming in compliance pain — FINIA regulation is new (2020) and most are still catching up
- they use ancient tools. many still run on excel + outlook + shared drives
- they have money. swiss finance WTP is absurd compared to consumer
- i have insider credibility — i work at one of these firms, i speak their language
- zurich is geographically tiny, i could literally walk to 200 of these firms

what i don't know:
- what specific pain point converts fastest? compliance reporting? client onboarding? portfolio monitoring? CRM?
- cold email or linkedin or phone or show up in person?
- german or english for outreach? (my german is fluent but my tech vocabulary is english)
- do i need a landing page first or can i sell before building?
- how do i handle the conflict of interest with my employer?
- what's the right sequence? 10 cold emails → 3 demos → 1 paying customer → build?

constraints:
- this is a side project alongside a full-time job
- no marketing budget, only time
- i can build fast but i can't do sustained social media content creation
- i need revenue validation before i build anything substantial
- i don't want to build a product nobody wants again

the dream: 10 paying customers at CHF 500/month = CHF 5000/month side income within 6 months
