# 🎯 Epic: prepper

## Business Value

This is a **structured research initiative**, not a product build. The goal is to develop a comprehensive understanding of the political dimensions of prepper movements in the United States and United Kingdom, benchmarked against existing knowledge of Swiss and German prepper culture. The value is personal knowledge capital — filling a specific gap in how Anglophone prepper movements differ politically from their Continental European counterparts.

The research has potential downstream value if it seeds a future content project or community tool, but that is **not assumed**. The immediate payoff is a well-organized, referenceable knowledge base that captures the political landscape — partisan alignment, legal frameworks, anti-government ideology, and cultural perception — across both countries. This prevents the common pattern of scattered reading that never consolidates into usable understanding.

If a product idea does emerge (community app, content platform, comparative analysis tool), this research becomes the domain-knowledge foundation that makes scoping realistic rather than speculative.

## Scope

### What This Epic Covers

- **US prepper political spectrum** — mapping the left-prepper, right-prepper, and apolitical-prepper segments, including militia-adjacent movements, urban progressive preppers, and civic-preparedness advocates
- **UK prepper political landscape** — how prepper culture operates under strict firearms legislation, its relationship to government emergency planning (Cabinet Office / COBRA frameworks), and whether it aligns with any political parties or movements
- **Comparative framework vs. Swiss/German models** — structured comparison on key axes: government integration, legal environment, partisan identity, media framing, and cultural legitimacy
- **Legal and policy dimensions** — gun laws, land use, stockpiling regulations, and how these shape prepper behavior differently across US, UK, Switzerland, and Germany

### What This Epic Does NOT Cover

- ❌ **Software product design** — no app, API, or UI is being built in this epic
- ❌ **Survivalist skills or gear reviews** — this is political analysis, not preparedness how-to
- ❌ **Historical deep-dives pre-2000** — focus is on the modern (post-9/11, post-2008, post-COVID) political landscape
- ❌ **Fringe conspiracy movements** — sovereign citizens, QAnon-adjacent groups are noted for context but not the research focus

## Tasks

| # | Task | Dependencies | Effort | Priority |
|---|------|--------------|--------|----------|
| 1 | **Map US prepper political segments** — identify and characterize 3-5 distinct political groupings (right-libertarian, left/mutual-aid, apolitical/civic, religious, militia-adjacent) with key organizations, media figures, and stated beliefs | None | 2 days | High |
| 2 | **Map UK prepper political landscape** — document how UK preppers organize under different legal constraints, their relationship to government emergency policy, and whether any political alignment exists (Brexit-linked, green-collapse, apolitical) | None | 2 days | High |
| 3 | **Document legal and policy frameworks** — compare US (federal + key state-level) and UK regulations on firearms, food stockpiling, off-grid living, radio communications, and how these laws shape prepper culture differently than Swiss/German civil-defense integration | Tasks 1, 2 | 1 day | High |
| 4 | **Build comparative framework** — create a structured comparison across US, UK, Switzerland, and Germany on 5 axes: government integration, legal permissiveness, partisan identity, media perception, and cultural legitimacy | Tasks 1, 2, 3 | 1 day | High |
| 5 | **Synthesize key insights** — distill findings into a referenceable summary: what is genuinely different about Anglophone prepper politics vs. DACH-region prepper culture, and what drives those differences | Task 4 | 1 day | Low |

Tasks 1 and 2 are **parallel** — no dependency between US and UK research tracks.

## Success Criteria

- ✅ US prepper landscape documented with at least 3 distinct political segments, each with named organizations or figures as evidence
- ✅ UK prepper landscape documented with clear explanation of how legal constraints (especially firearms) reshape the movement compared to the US
- ✅ Comparative framework produced covering all 4 countries (US, UK, Switzerland, Germany) across at least 4 political/cultural axes
- ✅ Each claim supported by at least one concrete example (organization, law, event, or public figure)
- ✅ Research is consolidated into a single referenceable document, not scattered notes
