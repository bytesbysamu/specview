# Portfolio Distribution Strategy — Brain Dump

## What this is

Distribution playbook for 4 live SaaS products on one VPS. Based on TrustMRR data, Marc Lou's portfolio model, and channel-specific tactics. The anchor is humaniz.me. The model is Marc Lou (cross-sell 14+ products from one brand), not Pieter Levels (lifestyle solo grind).

## Where we sit

68.9% of TrustMRR startups make $0-$1K/month. Median time to first $1 is 5 months. 33% of listed startups end up FOR SALE. We need to beat the median — that means focus, not spread.

## The portfolio

| Product | Market proof | Channel | Status |
|---------|-------------|---------|--------|
| humaniz.me | $3.7M ARR (Undetectable AI), $42K/30d (Lunchbreak) | TikTok + Reddit | Anchor bet |
| specview.dev | Spec Kit 104K stars, least contested niche | Show HN + r/ClaudeAI | Dev tool play |
| speedback.pro | Marker.io $39/mo ceiling, SeggWat in DE | Cold outreach DACH | Niche B2B |
| trendfy.me | Provamoda $229/30d, Outfii viral TikTok | TikTok outfit reveals | Consumer bet |

## The model — Marc Lou, not Levels

Marc Lou made $1,032,000 in 2025 from 14+ products. Cross-promotion via footer links on every product. ShipFast ($20K/mo) feeds DataFast feeds CodeFast. One-time pricing converts first, then upsell to subscription.

Our setup is structurally identical (4 products, one brand) but we need one anchor at $1K MRR before cross-sell economics work.

## 90-day structure

**Days 1-30:** All-in on humaniz.me distribution. TikTok daily, Reddit week 3, affiliate program week 2.

**Days 30-90:** Run the other 3 in parallel but smaller. One Show HN for specview. 20 cold emails/day for speedback. TikTok seeding for trendfy.

**Days 90-120:** Kill threshold. Any product at $0 MRR gets paused or listed FOR SALE on TrustMRR.

## Kill rules

- Product at $0 by day 120 → pause or sell
- humaniz.me at $0 by day 60 → distribution is wrong, not product — pivot niche
- Any product hits $1K MRR → implement footer cross-promotion across all 4

## What not to do

- Don't buy followers or use AI replies on X
- Don't ask for PH upvotes (against TOS, backfires)
- Don't launch all 4 on Product Hunt at once
- Don't hire before $5K MRR
- Don't polish landing pages for more than 2 weeks
