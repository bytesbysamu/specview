For any B2B SaaS targeting Swiss FINIA-licensed financial firms, these are the free distribution channels and prospect sources discovered through research on 2026-04-11:

## Prospect lists (free, downloadable)

- **FINMA register** — `finma.ch/en/authorisation/portfolio-managers-and-trustees/`. Look for "FLV ERVT" download. Contains every FINIA-licensed portfolio manager and trustee in Switzerland (~2,000 firms total). NOT YET verified whether the export includes contact emails or only firm names + addresses.
- **Infinity.swiss Treuhandverzeichnis** — `infinity.swiss/en/treuhandverzeichnis`. Official Swiss Treuhänder directory. Free directory listing, can be scraped for firm + contact info.

## Trade associations / distribution channels

- **VAPA (Verband unabhängiger Vermögensverwalter)** — `vapa.ch`. Trade association for independent Swiss wealth managers. Has member directory, newsletter, events. Sponsoring/speaking at VAPA events = direct access to hundreds of EAMs.
- **TreuhandSuisse** — trade association for the broader Swiss fiduciary/accounting profession. Member directory likely accessible.

## Sam-specific advantage: language coverage

Sam is fluent in German AND French. This is important: most Swiss FINIA-licensed firms are German-speaking (Zurich, Zug, central CH), but the Geneva/Lausanne/Romandy belt is French-speaking. Sam can write native-quality cold outreach in BOTH, which:
1. Excludes ~95% of indie hackers (US/EN-only) from competing
2. Signals "one of us" to Swiss buyers — half the sale in Swiss B2B
3. Doubles his addressable prospect universe vs an EN-only competitor

How to apply: When drafting cold outreach for Sam, default to a German version + a French version of every email/landing page. The German version is for Zurich/Zug/central CH; the French version is for Geneva/Lausanne. English is the fallback for international firms only.
