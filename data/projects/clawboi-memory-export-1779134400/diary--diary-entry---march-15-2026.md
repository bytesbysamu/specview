# Diary Entry - March 15, 2026

## Mood: 6/10

Sad, but clear. The kind of sad that doesn't cloud judgment. Closure achieved.

## What Happened

**Final talk with Lea today.**

We both knew this was it — the last conversation before she leaves for New Zealand on Thursday. Both of us were sad the relationship was over, but glad to have the conversation in person.

**It was a good discussion. Very open.**

She described how it went, how it got worse, what she thinks are the underlying issues. It was helpful to listen to how she sees the events and developments.

### Her Perspective

**Summer depression changed everything.**

She says she helped me a lot during that time. Afterwards, she switched to thinking "Sam can't ask for anything else pretty much." Like she'd already given her quota, was tapped out.

**On communication:**

She thinks I wasn't persistent enough. I communicated my wishes but never gave orders. She ignored wishes and thought that was okay, didn't think it would have any consequences.

That's the operating system difference right there. Wishes ≠ expectations in her mind. Only "orders" registered as things she had to respond to.

**In general, we're incompatible.**

She thinks she gave it all, thought about me a lot, cared a lot. Even though I find it difficult to understand how she can think that, I believe she believes it.

### What I Realized

**The breakup was a good thing, even though it hurts now.**

She was dressed well today — pretty much like I always suggested or wished. Too late, but it shows she *could* do it. She just didn't prioritize it when it mattered.

Part of both of us thought we might get back together.

**But I know the truth: I would be unhappy with her.**

My underlying trust in her has disappeared. A relationship held by rules and regulation, where I need to assert too much and/or warn with consequences, makes no sense for me. Means no happiness. And it's the wrong way to start a family or marry.

**Pretty much what I said didn't matter after summer.** And she agrees.

### The Ending

I thanked her for the talk. She wished me lots of success for the future.

Clean. Dignified. Done.

## What I'm Thinking About

**Trust is gone = relationship is over.**

No matter how much we cared, no matter how good Lyon was, no matter how sad we both are — you can't build a life with someone you don't trust.

I tried. I optimized. I gave chances. The data said no. December feelings were right.

**The dress detail sticks with me.**

She showed up dressed exactly how I always wished. Proves it wasn't about ability — it was about priorities. She *could* do the things I asked for, she just didn't think they mattered enough during the relationship.

Too late now. But at least I know it wasn't impossible for her, just not important enough.

**Part of me wanted to get back together today.**

I felt it. She probably felt it too. But that's grief + nostalgia, not data.

The real question is: Would I be happy with her? No.

Would I trust her with my ambitions, my family plans, my life? No.

Can I build the life I want with someone whose operating system requires rules and consequences to function? No.

**So it's done.**
