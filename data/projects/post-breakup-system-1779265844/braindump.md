# Post-Breakup Recovery System

just went through a breakup with lea. we were together and it ended in march. i've been doing okay but i notice patterns — i check her instagram, i replay conversations in my head, i have this urge to text her especially late at night. i set a boundary of 2+ months no contact but i already almost broke it twice.

what i actually want is a structured system for recovering. not therapy-speak bullshit, real actionable structure. like a protocol.

things that are working:
- gym is back on track, leg fully recovered
- social calendar is filling up (games nights, dinners, seeing friends)
- coding keeps my mind occupied, i shipped an entire app in 42 hours during the worst of it
- mornings are good, i wake up at 6:45 and feel clear

things that are NOT working:
- evenings alone are dangerous. that's when the rumination starts
- i still analyze every text exchange looking for hidden meaning
- i compare every new social interaction to what we had
- weekends with no plans = spiral risk

what i think i need:
1. a structured evening routine that fills the 7pm-11pm danger zone
2. a way to track progress that isn't just "days since contact"
3. clear criteria for when i'm actually ready to date again vs just lonely
4. a social expansion strategy — i need new people, not just existing friends
5. something to do with all the emotional energy besides coding

i live in zurich, 27, software engineer, decent friend group but it's been mostly couples recently. i speak german and english. i like sports, tech, going out, travel.

questions i don't know the answer to:
- should i actively try to meet new people or focus inward first?
- is the coding productivity a healthy coping mechanism or avoidance?
- how do i handle mutual friends who bring her up?
- what's the right timeline for this? everyone says "it takes time" but that's useless
