## Issue Breakdown

| Issue | Severity | Addressed By |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| Couples lack tools after finding a partner | CRITICAL | The Advisor, Guided Check-Ins |
| Relationship issues go unaddressed until crisis | CRITICAL | Guided Check-Ins, Structured Questions |
| Generic advice doesn't fit specific dynamics | HIGH | Structured Questions (context building) |
| WhatsApp-only tools limit accessibility | HIGH | Multiple Input Methods |
| Partners avoid difficult conversations | HIGH | Situation Description, The Advisor |
| No visibility into partner's perspective | HIGH | Guided Check-Ins (comparison feature) |
| Users forget past patterns and progress | MEDIUM | AI memory and context retention |
| Onboarding friction for new users | MEDIUM | Flexible input methods |
| Privacy concerns with chat uploads | MEDIUM | Free text/description alternatives |
| Users unsure how to articulate issues | LOW | Guided prompts and structured questions |
| Inconsistent check-in habits | LOW | Weekly/monthly cadence structure |

---

## Issues Not Addressed

| Issue | Reason |
|-------|--------|
| Partner refuses to participate | Single-user mode not explicitly defined |
| Crisis intervention needs | Out of scope — app is preventive, not emergency |
| Cultural communication differences | Lower priority for MVP |
