# RelateAI — Product Thesis

## One-Sentence Summary

The relationship app for people who already found someone — AI-powered communication insights to maintain and improve your relationship over time.

---

## Problem

**Who feels the pain**: Couples in committed relationships who want to communicate better but don't need (or can't afford) couples therapy.

**The pain itself**: Misunderstandings escalate into fights. The same conflicts repeat. Partners feel unheard but don't know why. There's no neutral third party to help decode what's actually happening.

**Why it matters**: Poor communication is the #1 predictor of relationship failure. Most couples wait 6 years too long before seeking help. By then, patterns are entrenched.

---

## Core Idea

Dating apps help you find someone. RelateAI helps you keep them.

Not a chat analyzer — a relationship companion that learns your dynamic over time through multiple input methods: conversation uploads, situation descriptions, guided check-ins, and structured assessments. Think of it as having a couples therapist in your pocket, grounded in real research (Gottman, attachment theory, NVC), but specific to YOUR relationship.

---

## Core Thesis

**The bet we're making**:

1. Couples will share relationship context with AI if it feels private, judgment-free, and genuinely helpful
2. Longitudinal data (patterns over time) creates a moat that single-use advice tools can't match
3. The real product is the accumulated understanding — not any single analysis

---

## Target Users

**Now (MVP)**: One partner seeking to improve communication (doesn't require both to start)

**Later**: Couples using together with independent check-ins and comparison views

---

## What Makes This Different

| Competitor | Gap | Our Advantage |
|------------|-----|---------------|
| Couples therapy | $150+/session, scheduling friction | Always available, $12/mo |
| Generic AI chat | No memory, no relationship context | Learns your dynamic over time |
| Relationship apps | Focus on date ideas, not communication | Deep on the hard stuff |
