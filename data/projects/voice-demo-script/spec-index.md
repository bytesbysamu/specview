# 📋 Voice Demo Script

> A 30-second screen recording script that proves Humaniz.me works — paste AI text, humanize it, speak into mic, humanize again — designed for maximum Reddit engagement.

## Overview

Humaniz.me is live in production but has zero social proof. The first Reddit post on r/ChatGPT needs a visual demo — text-only posts get buried while video demos consistently hit the front page. The difference between 50 views and 50,000 views is a 30-second screen recording that proves the product works.

This capability defines the exact script, text content, recording workflow, and post-production steps needed to produce a single high-impact demo video. The script follows a four-beat structure: (1) paste obviously AI-generated text, (2) tap Humanize and watch it transform, (3) tap the mic, speak a natural sentence, watch transcription fill the textarea, (4) tap Humanize on the spoken text for a polished result. Each beat is timed to land within 30 seconds total, with no dead air and no editing required beyond a single trim.

The deliverable is not just the script — it's a repeatable recording protocol. Once the first demo ships, the same structure can be reused for future product demos (Cold Email Writer, LinkedIn Post Generator) with only the sample text swapped out. The demo becomes a template.
