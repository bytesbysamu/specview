# RelateAI — Product Thesis

## One-Sentence Summary

A relationship companion that helps couples communicate better by turning conversations, conflicts, and check-ins into actionable insights grounded in relationship science.

## Problem

**Who feels the pain:** Couples in committed relationships (6 months to 10+ years) who want to stay together but struggle to communicate effectively.

**The pain itself:** 
- **67% of relationship conflicts are perpetual** — they never get "solved," only managed well or poorly
- **Most couples wait 6 years** before seeking professional help, by which point damage is often done
- **Therapy costs $150-300/session** and requires scheduling, logistics, and vulnerability that creates friction
- **Daily friction compounds silently** — the same fight about dishes, the text that felt cold, the topic you keep avoiding

**Why it matters:**
Relationships are the #1 predictor of happiness and health. Yet we have apps for fitness, finances, meditation, productivity — but nothing for the relationship itself. The tools that exist are either:
- Dating apps (wrong phase — you already found someone)
- Couples therapy (expensive, high-friction, reactive not proactive)
- Self-help books (generic, not personalized, no feedback loop)

The gap: **No ongoing, personalized, low-friction tool for relationship maintenance.**

## Core Idea

**The insight:** Relationships don't fail in dramatic moments. They fail in the accumulation of small miscommunications, unspoken resentments, and patterns neither person can see.

What couples need isn't more advice — it's a **mirror**. Something that:
- Reflects back what's actually happening in their communication
- Spots patterns they're blind to (criticism, stonewalling, defensiveness)
- Catches issues early, before they become crises
- Makes relationship work as habitual as checking your phone

**RelateAI is that mirror** — powered by decades of relationship science (Gottman, attachment theory, NVC) but delivered through modern AI that learns your specific dynamic.

## Core Thesis

**The bet we're making:**

1. **Input flexibility unlocks adoption.** Couples won't export WhatsApp consistently, but they *will* describe situations, do quick check-ins, and occasionally paste a heated exchange. Multiple input methods = sustained engagement.

2. **AI can deliver 80% of therapy value at 1% of the cost.** The Gottman Institute's research is public. Attachment theory is well-documented. Most couples don't need a human therapist — they need the frameworks applied to their specific situation, consistently.

3. **Couples who track improve.** Just like fitness apps create awareness that drives behavior change, relationship dashboards will create visibility into patterns that are currently invisible.

4. **The "Advisor" interaction model works.** People already talk to AI about personal problems. A specialized, context-aware relationship advisor — one that remembers your history and knows your patterns — will feel more valuable than generic ChatGPT.

5. **Prevention beats intervention.** The real market isn't troubled relationships seeking rescue. It's healthy-ish relationships wanting to stay that way.

## Target Users

**Now (MVP):**
- Couples aged 25-40 in relationships of 1-5 years
- Both partners moderately tech-comfortable
- At least one partner interested in "relationship work"
- Experiencing recurring friction but not in crisis
- Income: $75K+ household (willing to pay for relationship tools)

**Later:**
- Engaged couples (pre-marital prep)
- New parents (highest-stress relationship period)
- Long-distance couples (communication is everything)
- Couples in therapy (supplement between sessions)
- Enterprise: couples benefits for progressive employers

## What Makes This Different

| Competitor | What They Do | Why RelateAI Wins |
|------------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Couples therapy** | Expert human guidance | 100x cheaper, available 24/7, no scheduling friction |
| **Gottman Card Decks** | Static question prompts | Personalized to your dynamic, tracks progress over time |
| **Relish/Lasting** | Courses and exercises | Not a curriculum — it's an always-on advisor that responds to *your* situations |
| **ChatGPT** | Generic advice on request | Remembers your relationship, trained on research, structured for couples |
| **Paired/Honeydue** | Gamified couple activities | Deeper — not just "fun prompts" but real pattern analysis |

**The moat:** 
- Accumulated context about each couple (switching costs increase over time)
- Proprietary prompt engineering grounded in validated relationship research
- Dual-input comparison (both partners' perspectives create unique insights)

## Success Criteria

**Phase 1: Validation (0-3 months)**
- 500 couples try the product
- 100 couples use it weekly for 4+ weeks
- NPS > 40
- 5 couples willing to pay $10+/month

**Phase 2: Product-Market Fit (3-12 months)**
- 1,000 paying subscribers at $9-15/month
- Monthly churn < 8%
- 30% of users engage weekly
- Couples report measurable improvement in satisfaction

**Phase 3: Scale (12-24 months)**
- $50K MRR
- Organic growth > 30% of new users (word of mouth)
- Partnership with one therapist network or employer benefits program
