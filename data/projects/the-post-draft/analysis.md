## Hard Constraints

1. **Channel**: Twitter/X primary, LinkedIn secondary cross-post
2. **Length**: 10 tweets. Not 8, not 12. Ten.
3. **Tone**: War story with proof. Not a tutorial. Not a product launch announcement. A founder telling other founders what they built and what it cost.
4. **Proof**: Every claim backed by a number from `docs/the-post-numbers.md`. No "we built something amazing" without receipts.
5. **CTA**: Must include both landing page URL and TestFlight mention. One primary, one secondary.

## Open Questions

1. **Pattern name**: "Five-Part Agent" is the working name. Is it the shipping name? Alternatives: "Spec-to-Ship Pipeline", "The Five-Part Loop", "Document-First Agent". Decision required before Task 2.
2. **Landing page URL**: Is it humaniz.me or a dedicated spec-doc landing page? Needs to be resolved before the CTA tweet.
3. **TestFlight**: Which app is the TestFlight for — Bubls, Trendfy, or a spec-doc demo? The thread needs to point to something the reader can touch.

## Explicitly Out of Scope

- Building a landing page (must already exist)
- Recording a demo video (thread is text-only with optional screenshot)
- Drafting follow-up threads or content calendar
- LinkedIn-native content (only reformatted cross-post)
