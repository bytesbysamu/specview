## Overview

The Post Thread is a single, high-impact Twitter/X thread that names and explains the Five-Part Agent pattern — the system behind 175 commits, 18 epics, and 42 hours of autonomous AI execution. The thread is a war story aimed at technical founders, not a tutorial. It opens with hard numbers, walks through the arc from failure to system, names the pattern, shows proof, and closes with a CTA to the landing page and TestFlight.

The window for this post is narrow. The numbers are fresh, the pattern is unnamed in the wild, and the technical founder audience on Twitter is actively seeking agent orchestration content. Every day without publishing lets the numbers go stale and risks someone else naming a similar pattern first. The thread needs to land in one sitting — drafted, reviewed, and queued.

Cross-posting to LinkedIn is secondary. The Twitter thread is the canonical artifact; LinkedIn gets a reformatted version after the Twitter thread is live and gathering signal.
