## Issue Breakdown

| Issue | Severity | Addressed By |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| Couples lack objective perspective on communication patterns | CRITICAL | The Advisor, Conversation Upload |
| Relationship problems compound silently until crisis | CRITICAL | Guided Check-Ins |
| Generic relationship advice doesn't account for unique dynamics | HIGH | Structured Questions, contextual AI |
| People don't know how to start difficult conversations | HIGH | Situation Description input |
| Partners perceive same events differently without knowing it | HIGH | Guided Check-Ins (comparison view) |
| Therapy is expensive, inaccessible, or stigmatized | HIGH | Always-available Advisor |
| Text-based communication lacks tone/intent, causes misunderstanding | MEDIUM | Conversation analysis |
| People struggle to articulate relationship concerns clearly | MEDIUM | Multiple input methods |
| Relationship skills aren't taught; most people learn by failing | MEDIUM | Research-grounded coaching |
| Single-user relationship tools miss partner perspective | LOW | Independent partner check-ins |
| Screenshot/memory-based inputs are less accurate than exports | LOW | Not addressed |

---

## Issues Not Addressed

| Issue | Reason |
|-------|--------|
| Real-time communication coaching during live conversations | Out of scope for MVP |
| Couples therapy replacement for serious dysfunction | Out of scope — app is enhancement, not treatment |
| Accuracy variance in OCR/memory-based inputs | Lower priority — acceptable tradeoff for accessibility |
