# RelateAI — Product Thesis

## One-Sentence Summary

The relationship app for people who already found someone—AI-powered communication insights to maintain and improve what you've built.

---

## Problem

**Who feels the pain**: Couples in committed relationships (6 months to 20 years) who want to stay connected but don't know how to improve communication without therapy.

**The pain itself**: 
- Same arguments repeat without resolution
- Small disconnections compound into distance
- No feedback loop on communication patterns
- Therapy is expensive ($150+/session), stigmatized, and hard to schedule

**Why it matters**: 40-50% of marriages end in divorce. Most cite "communication issues." The tools exist for finding love, not keeping it.

---

## Core Idea

Dating apps optimize for matching. RelateAI optimizes for staying matched. A relationship companion that learns your dynamic over time—not generic advice, but insights specific to YOUR patterns, YOUR partner, YOUR history.

---

## Core Thesis

**The bet we're making**:

1. Couples will share relationship context with AI if it delivers genuinely useful, non-judgmental insights
2. Multiple input methods (chat uploads, situation descriptions, check-ins) make adoption natural—no single friction point
3. AI grounded in research (Gottman, attachment theory, NVC) can provide 80% of therapy value at 5% of the cost

---

## Target Users

**Now (MVP)**: Millennial/Gen-Z couples (25-40) in relationships of 1-5 years. Tech-comfortable, self-improvement oriented, therapy-curious but not actively in therapy.

**Later**: Engaged couples, newlyweds, long-term marriages seeking renewal, co-parents navigating separation.

---

## What Makes This Different

| Competitor | Gap | Our Advantage |
|------------|-----|---------------|
| Couples therapy | $600+/month, scheduling friction | Always available, $12/month |
| Relationship apps (Paired, Lasting) | Generic prompts, no personalization | Learns YOUR specific dynamic |
| ChatGPT | No memory, generic advice | Persistent context, research-grounded |
| Self-help books | One-size-fits-all | Adapts to your attachment styles, patterns |

---

## Success Criteria

| Phase | Target | Timeframe |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| Validation | 100 couples using weekly, 40% retention | Month 1-2 |
| PMF | $2K MRR, NPS > 50, users say "this helped my relationship" | Month 6 |
