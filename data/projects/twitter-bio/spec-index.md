## Overview

Twitter/X is the primary distribution channel for reaching technical founders, solo devs, and AI-augmented builders — the exact audience that would adopt spec-doc methodology. Currently there is zero presence on the platform, which means every reader of THE post who clicks through to a profile finds nothing. That's a leaky funnel at the highest-intent moment.

This capability creates the complete first-impression package: a bio that communicates the core thesis in under 160 characters, a header image that visually anchors the "specs, not chat" methodology, a pinned tweet thread that serves as the canonical introduction to the workflow, and the identity decisions (handle, name, photo) that make the profile feel intentional rather than hastily assembled.

The goal is not to build a content engine or a posting schedule. It is to ensure that the single highest-value touchpoint — someone landing on the profile after reading THE post — converts curiosity into a follow. Everything else (content cadence, engagement strategy, audience growth) is out of scope and belongs in a future capability.
