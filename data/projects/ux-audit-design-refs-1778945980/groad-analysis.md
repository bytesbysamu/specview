# Groad — Food Ordering System UI/UX Case Study

Source: Behance, 12 pages. PDF at docs/design-references/

## Design Process
Understand → Research → Sketch → Design → Implement → Evaluate

## Branding
- Logo: orange/green circular mark
- Primary: coral/salmon (#FF6B6B)
- Typography: SF Pro Text (Regular, Medium, Semibold)

## Key Screens
1. Onboarding (3 screens with food illustrations)
2. Auth: simple registration + OTP verification
3. Discovery: location-based restaurants, category chips, infinite scroll
4. Restaurant detail: hero image, menu items, customization
5. Cart & payment: multiple methods, color-coded cards, promo codes
6. Order tracking: real-time map, driver location, timeline
7. Rating: star rating + text review post-delivery
8. Driver interface: dark theme, map-centric, earnings dashboard

## Design Patterns
- Rounded corners (vs Specview's sharp)
- Card + shadow layouts (vs Specview's borders)
- Warm coral accent (vs Specview's slate blue)
- Bottom nav bar (mobile-first)
- Illustration-heavy onboarding
- Dark theme for driver/merchant dashboards
