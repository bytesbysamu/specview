# Life Routines Agent — The Non-Negotiable Weekly Structure

## Why this exists

From diary March 11: "Am I building or avoiding?" From diary March 3: "I want to be more organized, get things done, be social and active, be happy." From May 22 /reflect: career prep absent, diary gap of 68 days, gym zero mentions since March 3.

I have no weekly structure. When something is exciting (building products, social events), I go all-in. When it's routine (office, cleaning, gym, family visits), it drifts. The diary proves this: I can host a 6-person Ethiopian dinner (March 25) but can't maintain a gym habit for more than one day (March 3, never mentioned again).

I need a weekly skeleton — non-negotiable slots that the system tracks and nudges. Not a rigid calendar, but minimum commitments that keep the fundamentals running while I do everything else.

## The weekly minimums

### Office: 2-3x per week
I work at Finnofleet in finance. I should go to the office at least 2 times per week, ideally 3. Currently I'm probably going inconsistently — the diary never mentions "went to office" or "worked from home" explicitly. The system should:
- Ask in the daily diary check-in: "office or WFH today?"
- Track weekly count
- Nudge if below 2 by Thursday: "you've been to the office 1 time this week. Planning to go tomorrow?"
- This matters for: visibility at work (promotion/career), social connection with colleagues, routine

### Room cleanliness: always
My room should be clean at all times. Not spotless, but no dishes piling up, bed made, surfaces clear. The diary doesn't track this. The system should:
- Sunday evening prompt: "is your room clean going into the week?"
- Wednesday check: "mid-week reset — 10 minutes to tidy up"
- Before any social hosting: "having people over? Quick room check"
- Gamify: part of the discipline streak

  "This week: Office Mon/Wed/Thu. Gym: plan for Tue/Fri. Friends: nothing scheduled yet — who to reach out to? Family: last visit 12 days ago — this weekend?"

- Not a guilt machine — missed targets get a nudge, not a lecture
- Not separate from the financial agent — these routines ARE the financial agent's input
- Not a standalone app — everything flows through the existing diary → Telegram pipeline
