## The Core Problem

The relationship industry has a glaring blind spot: it's obsessed with matchmaking and ignores maintenance. Dating apps generate $8B+ annually helping people find partners, yet the far harder problem—keeping relationships healthy over years and decades—has no mainstream solution. Couples therapy exists but carries stigma, costs $150-300/hour, and most couples only try it when they're already in crisis. The result: preventable relationship deterioration becomes the norm.

Most couples don't fail due to incompatibility. They fail due to accumulated communication debt—small misunderstandings that compound, resentment that builds silently, patterns that calcify into dysfunction. By the time couples recognize something is wrong, they've often spent years reinforcing negative dynamics. There's no tool for the everyday maintenance work that healthy relationships require.

Consider: We have fitness trackers for physical health, meditation apps for mental health, and budgeting tools for financial health. Relationship health—arguably the strongest predictor of life satisfaction—has nothing except expensive professionals and self-help books that people rarely finish.

## Symptoms

Users experience:
- Repeating the same arguments without resolution or insight into why
- Feeling unheard by partners but unable to articulate what's missing
- Avoiding difficult conversations until they become explosive
- Lacking vocabulary to describe relationship dynamics or their own needs
- No baseline for "normal"—unsure if their relationship is healthy or not
- Communication patterns learned from dysfunctional families that they can't unlearn alone
- Wanting to improve but finding therapy too expensive, too stigmatized, or too unavailable
- Post-fight confusion about what actually happened and what went wrong
- Losing track of positive moments while negative patterns dominate memory
- Partners having completely different perceptions of the same interaction
- No early warning system for drifting apart
- Feeling like they're failing at something everyone else seems to manage naturally

## Issue Breakdown

### Critical Issues

| Issue | Evidence | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| No accessible relationship maintenance tool exists | Dating apps: $8B industry. Relationship maintenance: scattered self-help books and expensive therapy | The gap between finding and keeping has zero product coverage |
| Couples only seek help in crisis | 70% of couples wait 6+ years before seeking therapy; by then 50% have irreversible damage | Prevention market is unaddressed |
| Communication insight is locked behind $200/hour experts | Gottman-trained therapists are scarce and expensive; average person has no access to evidence-based guidance | Knowledge exists but distribution is broken |

### High Priority Issues

| Issue | Evidence | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| No longitudinal view of relationship health | Couples can't track patterns over time; rely on biased memory | Memory favors recent events and negative patterns |
| Generic advice fails individual dynamics | "Communicate more" doesn't help when the actual pattern is pursuer-distancer | Every couple has unique dysfunction fingerprint |
| Input friction kills adoption | Chat analyzers require WhatsApp exports; most people just want to describe a situation | Technical barriers block the people who need help most |
| Partners have asymmetric relationship awareness | One partner often sees problems the other doesn't; no neutral ground for comparison | Creates "crazy-making" dynamic where concerns get dismissed |
| No framework for difficult conversations | People know WHAT they need to say but not HOW | Lack of scripts/approaches causes avoidance |

### Medium Priority Issues

| Issue | Evidence | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Attachment and communication styles are invisible | Most people don't know their attachment style or how it affects conflicts | Self-awareness gap prevents targeted improvement |
| Positive moments go untracked | Negativity bias means fights are memorable, small kindnesses aren't | Skewed perception of relationship health |
| Check-in habits don't exist | No prompting mechanism for regular relationship reflection | Maintenance happens only when problems surface |
| Context gets lost in generic tools | ChatGPT can discuss relationships but forgets everything; no continuity | Each conversation starts from zero |

## Issues NOT Addressed

| Issue | Reason |
|-------|--------|
| Abusive relationship detection and intervention | Requires professional crisis resources; out of scope for v1 |
| Couples therapy replacement for severe dysfunction | Tool is for maintenance and mild-moderate issues; severe cases need human professionals |
| Matchmaking or partner finding | Explicitly positioned as post-dating; different market entirely |
| Multi-partner/polyamorous relationship dynamics | Complexity requires specialized approach; consider for future version |
| Family relationships beyond romantic partners | Different dynamics; potential future product extension |
| Legal or financial relationship issues | Requires professional expertise in different domains |
