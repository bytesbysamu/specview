## Overview

THE Post is a single long-form war story built from the numbers of one extraordinary development session: 6 epics, 32 tasks, 102 commits, 9,662 lines, 289 tests, zero regressions — all generated from brain dumps through a pipeline of parallel AI agents. It is not a tutorial. It is not a thought-piece. It is a receipts-first narrative that earns credibility through specificity and ends with a conversion tail: landing page URL + TestFlight invite.

The post serves three audiences simultaneously. For **technical founders**, the methodology story — brain dump to shipped product in hours — is the hook. For **AI builders**, the agent orchestration pattern (working title: "Five-Part Agent") is the takeaway. For **potential Bubls users**, the proof that the product works is the invitation to try it. One post, three funnels, one CTA.

Timing is load-bearing. The numbers are fresh. The session just happened. Every day between the session and the post dilutes urgency and makes the story feel retrospective instead of live. This capability treats the post as a time-sensitive distribution event, not an evergreen content piece. Ship it while the numbers are warm.
