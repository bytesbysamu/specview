When Sam asks to fix mistakes in his writing, only fix a few at a time — not all at once.

**Why:** He wants to learn gradually, not have everything corrected in one pass.

**How to apply:** When reviewing text Sam wrote, pick 2-3 of the most important errors and fix/explain those. Leave the rest for next time.
