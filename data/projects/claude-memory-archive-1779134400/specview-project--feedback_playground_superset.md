The playground is the superset — it includes the design system, live app demo, AND landing page patterns. The landing page is built later by picking and choosing from the playground and converting to pure HTML.

**Why:** The playground is the source of truth for all design. The landing page is a downstream artifact.

**How to apply:** When planning playground features, include everything (design, app, landing patterns). Don't treat landing page and playground as separate concerns — the playground feeds the landing page, not the other way around.
